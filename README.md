## 🌍 Why Dr. Interested Exists

Across Canada and globally, millions of young people move through school without meaningful career education.

Over **4 million youth** are navigating their futures, yet **less than half know their basic rights**, including the **right to development and education**. Career pathways in healthcare are often reduced to titles, grades, and prestige, instead of understanding systems, options, and impact.

Dr. Interested exists because that isn’t enough.

We believe career education should be early, honest, accessible, and empowering. Especially in healthcare, where curiosity and care matter as much as credentials.

---

## 📊 Our Impact

Dr. Interested has grown into a global platform reaching and supporting youth at scale.

• **70,000+ youth directly impacted** through events, programs, and initiatives  
• **1,000,000+ reached** through digital education and media  
• **1,200+ members** across **80+ countries**  
• **200+ youth leaders** running programs, research, and advocacy  
• **11+ major events** including conferences, research competitions, and wellness initiatives  

Impact isn’t a buzzword here. It’s the outcome of access.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

• Healthcare focused career education beyond just becoming a doctor  
• Youth centered events and conferences  
• Research, innovation, and policy engagement  
• Mental health and wellness advocacy  
• Leadership development and global collaboration  

We edge career education forward by explaining pathways, amplifying youth voices, and removing unnecessary barriers.
